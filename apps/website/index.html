FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Gloows365 free?

Yes — you can create an account and start learning on Gloows365 for free.

Which classes are supported?

Gloows365 currently supports students in Classes 6 to 12.

Which boards are supported?

CBSE, ICSE, State Boards, and other boards are supported.

Does Gloows365 support Indian languages?

Yes — Gloows365 is available across 12 Indian languages.

Is Gloows365 available on Android?

Gloows365 is available today as a web experience — sign up and start learning from any browser.

Is Gloows365 for parents or students?

Both. Students use Gloows365 to learn, practice and grow, while parents can trust it as a structured, education-first experience.

What is AI Guru?

AI Guru is Gloows365's AI-powered learning companion, designed to help students ask questions, explore concepts and get learning assistance.

What is Vidya Star?

Vidya Star is an MCQ-based knowledge challenge that turns revision into an engaging activity.

What are Educational Reels?

Educational Reels are short videos designed to make useful knowledge easy to learn in seconds.
